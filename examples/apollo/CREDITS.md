# Little West Crater frames

NASA Apollo 11 Hasselblad photographs AS11-40-5958 through AS11-40-5961, taken on the lunar surface by Neil Armstrong. The source files are the NASA Johnson Space Center Gateway to Astronaut Photography of Earth high-resolution scans. Each record in [sources.json](sources.json) contains the original download URL and SHA-256 digests of the original and bundled files.

The four example images are reduced derivatives. Processing retained rows from 8% to 91% of source height and columns from 5.5% to 91.5% of source width, resized to 690 pixels wide at the cropped aspect ratio, and encoded as JPEG at OpenCV quality 87. The preview in the README was produced by this repository. Dark margins outside the reconstructed coverage are a display matte.
